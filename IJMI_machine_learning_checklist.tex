\section*{Checklist notes}
The study is a retrospective single-split evaluation and is not a prospective impact or randomized implementation study. Calibration, subgroup performance, and external transportability require prospective and multi-site confirmation before clinical deployment. The intended use is decision support and risk stratification, not autonomous diagnosis or treatment selection.
